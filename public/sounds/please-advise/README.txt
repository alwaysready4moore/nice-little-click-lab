- Generate 3 to 5 variations of each and choose the quietest, cleanest version.

- Avoid cinematic bass, large room reverb, speech, background music, and sharp notification alarms.
- Export as MP3.
- Normalize the set so no single effect is dramatically louder than the others.
- Test with headphones and laptop speakers. The sounds should feel pleasant after ten rounds, not merely impressive once.
